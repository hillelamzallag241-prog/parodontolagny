Our mission

Your periodontal health, our daily commitment

Parodonto'Lagny was born from a conviction: the health of your gums deserves specialised attention, in a caring and attentive environment. Too many patients suffer from periodontal disease without even knowing it — and yet, solutions exist at every stage of the disease.

Our approach is built above all on collaboration between you and our team. We believe that a well-informed patient recovers better. That is why we take the time to explain your diagnosis, show you the results and involve you in every decision about your treatment.

Dr. Rebecca Cohen, trained in periodontology at the University of Nice-Sophia-Antipolis, cares for you with both compassion and rigour in Lagny-sur-Marne. Her philosophy: combining scientific rigour with a human, accessible approach, so that every visit is a step towards a lasting smile.

Support at every stage

Understand the disease

Gingivitis, periodontitis... Learn to recognise the early warning signs, understand how these diseases develop and the impact they can have on your overall health. The more you understand, the better you can act.

Tailored treatment

Every mouth is different, and so is every treatment. We favour gentle, non-surgical techniques as a first approach, with a care plan built around your diagnosis and your needs.

Periodontitis is a chronic disease that requires regular follow-up. We stay by your side after treatment to watch over your gums and preserve the results achieved, over the long term.
