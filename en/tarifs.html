A personalised approach

Every situation is unique

The cost of periodontal treatment depends on several factors specific to each patient: the stage of the disease, how widespread it is, the type of care required and the length of follow-up. This is why it is impossible to give a single standard fee that would apply to everyone.

During your first consultation, we carry out a complete assessment of your situation. Afterwards, we provide you with a detailed, personalised estimate, with a clear explanation of each proposed procedure. You know exactly what is planned, why, and at what cost. No surprises.

Our commitment: full transparency. We take the time to answer all your questions about fees and coverage options before starting any treatment.

Good to know

Coverage by the French national health insurance

Did you know that certain general medical conditions entitle you to coverage of periodontal care by the French national health insurance (Assurance Maladie)?

These conditions share a common inflammatory component. Chronic gum inflammation (periodontitis) can destabilise these conditions and worsen their symptoms. Conversely, these conditions increase the risk and severity of periodontitis. This is why the health insurance recognises the importance of treating periodontal disease in these patients and covers part of the care.

The conditions concerned notably include:

  • Diabetes (type 1 and type 2)
  • Heart failure, arrhythmias and severe congenital valvular heart disease
  • Coronary heart disease
  • HIV
  • Rheumatoid arthritis
  • Severe spondyloarthritis

If you are affected by one of these conditions, please let us know during your consultation. We will guide you through the coverage process.
